Bio

Who is Minaw?

Minaw is an independent singer/songwriter from Manila, Philippines.

He released 12 songs under the Citypop genre, mainly inspired by the retro and funky music of Tatsuro Yamashita, Mariya Takeuchi, and Miki Matsubara to mention a few.

His newest City Pop single, Obb, has been released on October 10, 2025.
